Watch out for noise

The channel is the path between the transmitter and the receiver, like air, wires, cable, or a mail route. Noise is anything that gets in the way and changes or blocks the message: static, a weak signal, background sound, a typo, or a crackly speaker. Noise isn't one of the six parts, but it can happen anywhere along the way.
